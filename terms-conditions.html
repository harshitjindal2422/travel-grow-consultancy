Terms & Conditions

Understand the advisory framework and booking conditions of Travel Grow Consultancy.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Travel Grow Consultancy

1. Agency Advisory Relationship

Travel Grow Consultancy acts as an independent travel advisor connecting clients with licensed airlines, boutique hotels, and ground transport partners. All quotes shared on our website and via consultation are indicative until confirmed with formal customer authorization.

2. Payment & Confirmation

Bookings are formally confirmed only after customer approval of the official written proposal and receipt of required advance payments as specified in your quotation document. All pricing is quoted in Indian Rupees (₹).

3. Passports & Visas

For international destinations, travellers are responsible for holding a passport with at least 6 months validity from departure and securing necessary visas unless visa documentation assistance is specifically agreed in the quotation scope.
