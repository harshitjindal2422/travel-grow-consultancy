THE HUMAN-ASSISTED JOURNEY

How We Craft Your Journey

A transparent 4-step human-assisted workflow designed specifically for Indian travellers. No generic bot packages, zero unexpected markups, and all pricing in Indian Rupees (₹).

01
STEP 1: INTAKE & DISCOVERY

Submit Your Travel Vision & Dates

Share where you want to travel, approximate dates, party size, and style preferences via our website enquiry forms, phone, or WhatsApp. Tell us if you prefer heritage properties, private pool villas, vegetarian cuisine, or child-friendly paces.

02
STEP 2: RESEARCH & CURATION

Manual Route & Consolidator Research

A senior travel curator manually inspects airline route matrices, checks consolidator seats to eliminate middleman fees, verifies hotel room views, and arranges vetted local chauffeurs.

03
STEP 3: TRANSPARENT PROPOSAL

Review Your Itemized INR (₹) Quotation

Within 24 business hours, you receive a clear, personalized proposal with day-by-day itineraries, exact hotel names, inclusions, and transparent pricing in Indian Rupees (₹). You can adjust any detail until it fits your expectations.

04
STEP 4: CONFIRMATION & 24/7 CARE

Seamless Booking & On-Trip Assistance

Once you approve, we secure your tickets and vouchers. Throughout your vacation, you enjoy 24/7 WhatsApp and phone access to your assigned advisor for hassle-free travel.
