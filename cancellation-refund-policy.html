Cancellation & Refund Policy

Transparent guidelines for itinerary changes, cancellations, and refund timelines in Indian Rupees (₹).

Last Updated: October 2026 • Travel Grow Consultancy

1. Standard Land Package Cancellation Schedule

  • More than 15 days before travel: Full refund of land package advance (less minimal bank transaction gateway fees).
  • 7 to 14 days before travel: 50% cancellation fee on land package components.
  • Less than 7 days before travel: Non-refundable due to contractual hotel and transport commitments.

2. Flight Tickets

Airline tickets are strictly governed by individual carrier fare rules. Refundable fares are credited back to the original payment source once disbursed by the airline. Travel Grow Consultancy charges zero hidden administrative cancellation fees.

3. Refund Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are processed to your original Indian bank account or credit card within 5 to 7 business days.
